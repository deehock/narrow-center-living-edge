# Brand Brief — Narrow Center / Living Edge

## Brand
**Narrow Center / Living Edge**

## Tagline
Govern fewer things absolutely. Observe many things continuously.

## Audience
Visa strategy operators, platform leaders, AI governance teams, and builders managing fleets of semi-autonomous agents that can speak, spend, access tools, remember, and delegate.

## Use case
A practical field guide for deciding what belongs in the common rails of an AI agent fleet and what should be left free at the edge.

## Personality
Institutional, restrained, strange enough to be remembered. More field manual than SaaS. More payment rails than dashboard. Primitive glyphs, ledgers, operating charters, and network maps.

## Avoid
Generic AI gradients, glowing neon, mascot illustrations, vague ethics language, over-carded dashboards, cyberpunk styling.
